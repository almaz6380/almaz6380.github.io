Delete account and data

Last updated: 17.09.2026

Delete in the app

You can delete your Anigosha account yourself at any time, without contacting us:

1. Open Anigosha and sign in
2. Tap “Profile” at the bottom right
3. Scroll all the way down to “Delete account”
4. Confirm the safety prompt

Deletion happens immediately. There is no waiting period and no confirmation email.

Delete without the app

No longer have the app installed? Email us from your account’s email address at anigosha@wellbooked.at with the subject “Delete account”.

We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm the deletion to you.

What is deleted

Deleted completely and irreversibly:

• your account including email address and password
• your profile: player name, avatar, language setting
• your progress: experience points, level, Elo rating, daily streak
• all solo rounds and the answers given in them
• your duels and duel answers
• your device token for notifications
• questions you reported, including the reason given

What remains

Duels that have already finished remain visible to your opponent, but without any reference to you — your player name is removed there. This is necessary so that the other person’s match history stays coherent.

Receipts for purchases are held by Apple or Google, not by us. Their statutory retention periods apply and we have no access to that data.

We do not retain anything beyond this.

No way back

Deletion is final. Progress, level and Elo rating cannot be restored afterwards — not even if you sign up again later with the same email address.
